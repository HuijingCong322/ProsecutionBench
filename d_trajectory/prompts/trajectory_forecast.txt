### SYSTEM
You forecast how U.S. patent prosecution will continue. You read the public record of one application up to a cutoff date and return calibrated probabilities as a json object only.

### USER
Estimate how the prosecution of this U.S. utility application will continue after the cutoff date {cutoff_date}.

Inputs
- Filing date: {filing_date}. Art unit: {art_unit}.
- First Office Action on the merits (O1): {o1_code} mailed {o1_date}.
- The applicant's reply to O1 (A1) was filed on {cutoff_date}. This is the cutoff: nothing dated after it is available.
- Prosecution state at the cutoff: {state}
- Prosecution record through the cutoff, oldest first. Each document starts with a header line [doc_id | document code | date | role | text source]. The text is machine-extracted, so ignore spacing, line-break and recognition artifacts.

{history}

Task
For each month m = 1, 2, ..., 24 after the cutoff, give the probability that, by the end of month m, the FIRST of these two events has happened:
- allowance: the USPTO mails a Notice of Allowance;
- abandonment: the USPTO mails a notice that the application is abandoned (for example, failure to reply to an Office Action, express abandonment, or abandonment after an appeal).
Only the first of the two counts, so they are competing events. Further Office Actions, advisory actions, interviews, requests for continued examination (RCE), appeals and petitions are NOT events; prosecution simply continues through them.

Procedural facts
- A reply to an Office Action is due within 3 months, extendable to 6 months with fees. If no reply is filed, the application becomes abandoned and the notice is usually mailed some weeks later.
- After a final rejection the applicant typically files an RCE, an appeal, an after-final reply, or lets the application go abandoned.

Rules
- Use only the supplied documents. Do not use anything you may remember about this application or patent.
- F_allowance and F_abandonment are cumulative: each list must be non-decreasing in m.
- For every month, F_allowance[m] + F_abandonment[m] must be at most 1. The remainder is the probability that neither event has happened yet.
- Express uncertainty through the probabilities.

Output
Return a json object only, with exactly these keys. Each list has 24 numbers between 0 and 1; position 1 is month 1 and position 24 is month 24.
{"F_allowance": [24 numbers], "F_abandonment": [24 numbers], "rationale": "at most three sentences"}
