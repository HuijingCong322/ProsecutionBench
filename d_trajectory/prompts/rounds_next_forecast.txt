### SYSTEM
You forecast how U.S. patent prosecution will continue. You read the public record of one application up to a cutoff date and return calibrated estimates as a json object only.

### USER
Estimate how the prosecution of this U.S. utility application will continue after the cutoff date {cutoff_date}.

Inputs
- Filing date: {filing_date}. Art unit: {art_unit}.
- First Office Action on the merits (O1): {o1_code} mailed {o1_date}.
- The applicant's reply to O1 (A1) was filed on {cutoff_date}. This is the cutoff: nothing dated after it is available.
- Prosecution state at the cutoff: {state}
- Prosecution record through the cutoff, oldest first. Each document starts with a header line [doc_id | document code | date | role | text source]. The text is machine-extracted, so ignore spacing, line-break and recognition artifacts.

{history}

Task 1: remaining rounds
How many further Office Actions will the USPTO mail after the cutoff, before the first Notice of Allowance or abandonment notice?
- Count only non-final rejections and final rejections mailed after the cutoff. O1 is already in the past and does not count.
- Do not count advisory actions, restriction requirements, Ex parte Quayle actions, interview summaries, notices of allowance, abandonment notices, or examiner's answers on appeal.
- A request for continued examination (RCE) does not end prosecution: rejections mailed after an RCE do count.
- 0 means the next disposition (allowance or abandonment) comes without any further rejection.
Give a probability for each count (0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 or more; the five numbers sum to 1) and your single best estimate as a whole number.

Task 2: time to the next examiner communication
How many days after the cutoff will the USPTO mail its next communication of any of these kinds: non-final rejection, final rejection, advisory action, restriction requirement, Ex parte Quayle action, Notice of Allowance, or abandonment notice? Interview summaries and other letters do not count.
Give your single best estimate in whole days.

Procedural facts
- A reply to an Office Action is due within 3 months, extendable to 6 months with fees. If no reply is filed, the application becomes abandoned and the notice is usually mailed some weeks later.
- After a final rejection the applicant typically files an RCE, an appeal, an after-final reply, or lets the application go abandoned.

Rules
- Use only the supplied documents. Do not use anything you may remember about this application or patent.
- Express uncertainty through the probabilities.

Output
Return a json object only, with exactly these keys:
{"p_further_actions": {"0": p, "1": p, "2": p, "3": p, "4+": p}, "further_actions": whole number, "days_to_next_action": whole number, "rationale": "at most three sentences"}
